# Health, safety and liability notice

**Read this before running X3Trainer or building anything from this
repository.**

## This is sample software

X3Trainer is a **demonstration and reference project** — published to show how
an AR heads-up display, a Bluetooth telemetry chain and a 3D exercise coach can
be built for smart glasses. It is **not a finished consumer product, not a
medical device, and not a certified fitness product.** It has not been reviewed,
tested or approved by any medical, clinical or regulatory body.

**It is not intended to be used to conduct actual exercise.**

## Consult a physician first

**Consult a qualified physician or licensed healthcare provider before
beginning any exercise program, and before using this software in connection
with any physical activity.** This is especially important if you:

- are over 35, pregnant, or have been inactive for some time;
- have or may have any heart, lung, circulatory, blood-pressure, metabolic,
  joint, bone, muscular or neurological condition;
- take any medication, particularly one affecting heart rate or blood pressure;
- have ever experienced chest pain, dizziness, fainting, or shortness of breath
  during or after exertion;
- are recovering from any injury, illness, surgery or procedure; or
- have any reason whatsoever to doubt your fitness to exercise.

If you have not been cleared by a physician, **do not use this software for
exercise.**

## Nothing here is medical advice

The timers, target heart-rate zones, target cadence, target pace, calorie
figures, spoken coaching, exercise selections, poses and stretches in this
project are **general information only**. They are **not** medical advice,
diagnosis, treatment, prescription, or a professional exercise prescription,
and they are not tailored to you. Nothing here should be relied on to make any
decision about your health.

**Never disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice because of
anything this software displays or says.**

## The measurements are estimates, not clinical data

Heart rate is read from a consumer wearable over Bluetooth and may be
inaccurate, delayed, or absent. Calories burned are a **rough statistical
estimate** derived from heart rate or an assumed activity intensity — they are
not a measurement of your actual energy expenditure. Cadence and speed come
from phone sensors and GPS and are approximations. Heart-rate zones are derived
from a user-entered maximum heart rate that is itself typically an
age-based estimate.

**None of these values are suitable for any medical, diagnostic or safety
purpose.** Do not use them to judge whether exertion is safe for you.

## Stop immediately if something is wrong

Stop exercising at once and seek medical attention if you experience pain,
chest pressure or tightness, dizziness, faintness, nausea, irregular heartbeat,
unusual shortness of breath, or any other concerning symptom. **In an emergency,
call your local emergency number.** Do not wait for the software to tell you
anything — it cannot detect a medical emergency and must never be relied on to.

## Displays worn on the face carry their own risks

This software drives a heads-up display worn over your eyes. Wearing a display
while moving can obscure or distract from hazards. **Do not use it while
driving or operating machinery**, and remain alert to traffic, terrain,
obstacles and other people at all times. The clear centre region is a design
choice, not a safety guarantee.
